Yacht Hydrodynamics Theory

Holtrop–Mennen Model & ITTC Formulations

This computational suite calculates bare-hull resistance components for yachts across displacement and semi-displacement speed profiles. Total hydrodynamic resistance ($R_t$) is separated into viscous friction and wave-making residuary components.

Total Bare-Hull Resistance
$$R_t = R_f + R_r$$

Rf represents viscous skin friction and Rr denotes wave residuary forces.

Viscous Skin Friction ($R_f$)

Frictional scaling uses the ITTC-1957 correlation line. It maps wetted surface area ($S$) against Reynolds number ($Re$).

ITTC-1957 Friction Line
$$R_f = \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 S C_f$$

Driven by:

$$C_f = \frac{0.075}{(\log_{10}(Re) - 2)^2}$$
