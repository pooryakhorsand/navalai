Hydrodynamics Standard

Wageningen B-Series Analysis

Mathematical verification of open-water performance characteristics using standardized polynomial calculations scaled by exact Reynolds corrections.

Open-water performance is plotted as non-dimensional coefficients across variations of the advance coefficient \(J\):

Advance Coefficient Equation
\[ J = \frac{V_A}{nD} \]

Relates the velocity of advance \(V_A\) to rotational frequency \(n\) and propeller diameter \(D\).

Thrust & Torque Coefficients
\[ K_T = \frac{T}{\rho n^2 D^4} \]
\[ K_Q = \frac{Q}{\rho n^2 D^5} \]
\[ 10K_Q = 10\left(\frac{Q}{\rho n^2 D^5}\right) \]

These coefficients are evaluated dynamically using the standard Wageningen B-Series regression polynomials.

Open-Water Propulsion Efficiency
\[ \eta_0 = \frac{J\,K_T}{2\pi K_Q} \]

Represents the ratio between useful propulsive power output and shaft power input.

Valid Regression Domain

\( Z = 2 \text{ to } 7 \) blades  ·  \( A_E/A_O = 0.30 \text{ to } 1.05 \)  ·  \( P/D = 0.50 \text{ to } 1.40 \)  ·  \( Re = 2\times10^6 \text{ to } 9\times10^6 \)

Curves are truncated at the first advance ratio where \(K_T\) or \(K_Q\) leaves the validated regression range, instead of being extrapolated beyond the documented domain.
