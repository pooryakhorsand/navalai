Williamson Turn — Precise MOB Recovery

Three-phase track-retracing manoeuvre — Abkowitz / Norrbin formulation

The Williamson Turn is the most accurate of the IMO-recommended man-overboard recovery trials. Unlike swift direct turns, it physically retraces the vessel's original track line in reverse, making it highly effective when the casualty is out of direct visual sight.

Three-Phase Rudder Schedule
$$ \delta_{cmd}(t) = \begin{cases} +35^\circ, & \psi < +60^\circ \quad \text{(Phase 1: Initial Turn)} \\ -35^\circ, & \psi \geq +60^\circ,\; \psi > -130^\circ \quad \text{(Phase 2: Counter Rudder)} \\ 0^\circ, & \psi \leq -130^\circ \quad \text{(Phase 3: Straight Retrace)} \end{cases} $$

The thresholds ($+60^\circ$ and $-130^\circ$) are mathematically calibrated to align the final reciprocal heading ($180^\circ$) exactly with the original baseline track.

Key Evaluation Metrics

The success of the track-retracing maneuver is evaluated using five vital dynamic values:

Recovery Quality Indicators
$$ t_1, \quad t_2, \quad t_{opp}, \quad \Delta y, \quad \Delta\psi $$

t₁ & t₂ — Phase transition timestamps. topp — reciprocal heading crossing time. Δy — final cross-track offset error. Δψ — heading deviation from reciprocal target.

Slew-Rate Rudder Dynamics

The physical limits of steering gears are integrated; commanded inputs transition at a finite rate, introducing system lags that are visible in the rudder telemetry curves.

Phase 1 Hard Rudder Turn

Hard rudder commands ($+35^\circ$) initiate the turn until heading reaches $+60^\circ$. Rendered in Amber.

Phase 2 Counter Swing

Rudder swings hard opposite ($-35^\circ$) to check the heading rate down to $-130^\circ$. Rendered in Coral.

Phase 3 Straight Return

Rudder centers to $0^\circ$ as the vessel slips back along the reciprocal track. Rendered in Teal.
